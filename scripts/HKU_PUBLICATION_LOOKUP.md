# Publication discovery and verification

Initial review window: **9 July–6 September 2026, inclusive**. The adjacent `hku_publication_leads.json` is a follow-up queue, not dated activity evidence. Its `verifiedAt` timestamps describe checks performed, not publication-date approval. Neither queued lead belongs in verified counts yet.

## Repeatable bounded recipe

1. Freeze the official roster and use canonical profile URLs as person IDs. Search publisher-deposited [Crossref metadata](https://api.crossref.org/works) using each person's name variants. A bounded first pass is 100 relevance-ranked records per query; log the query, returned count, total results and truncation. This is candidate discovery, not an exhaustive publication inventory. Crossref author queries match name tokens rather than exact identities; common names require particularly careful review.
2. Union separate candidate searches for publication dates in the reporting window and metadata created/indexed in the window. A publication-date-only query can miss an already-online article assigned to a future journal issue. Example live-tested queries: [Eric Schuldenfrei, creation window](https://api.crossref.org/works?query.author=Eric%20Schuldenfrei&filter=from-created-date:2026-07-09,until-created-date:2026-09-06&rows=100) and [Hongshan Guo, publication window](https://api.crossref.org/works?query.author=Hongshan%20Guo&filter=from-pub-date:2026-07-09,until-pub-date:2026-09-06&rows=100). Do not AND both date windows in one query: that is not their union. For monthly incremental refresh, use `from-index-date` with an overlap and deduplicate by DOI. Indexing or redeposit is never itself a new publication event.
3. Inspect actual author entries, affiliation, institutional records and verified ORCID-to-profile links. Names or research topics alone must not approve common-name matches. An [ORCID-filtered query](https://api.crossref.org/works?filter=orcid:0000-0002-1921-395X,from-created-date:2026-07-09,until-created-date:2026-09-06&rows=100) works, but this example ORCID remains a candidate identity link, not an approved binding. ORCID filters can also miss records lacking that identifier.
4. Retrieve each candidate's `/works/{DOI}` record and inspect the publisher's article history or an institutional version-of-record record. Preserve first-online, issue/print, acceptance, repository accession and metadata timestamps separately, with date precision and evidence URL. Prefer the first-online journal version-of-record date for journal-publication activity; separately label earlier preprints and corrections. Do not claim this is the first public appearance of the underlying research. Promote only when person linkage, event type and date are supported; otherwise retain the lead with a caveat.

[Crossref's official API documentation](https://github.com/Crossref/rest-api-doc) documents date filters, fuzzy author-field queries and incremental indexing. Missing days/months are internally interpreted as the earliest possible date: retain month/year precision and do not convert this convenience into an asserted calendar day. A September-only record is not proof of publication by 6 September.

## Additional sources and observed failure modes

- [HKU Architecture publication gateway](https://www.arch.hku.hk/research/publications/) links to the [Scholars Hub collection](https://hub.hku.hk/handle/10722/38526). Review `dc.date.issued` separately from `dc.date.accessioned`; “Recent Submissions” can surface old research uploaded recently. For example, [Ying Zhou's market-building article](https://hub.hku.hk/handle/10722/374891) was issued 17 April 2026 but accessioned 11 August.
- Publisher RSS is accessible without an API key: [Cities](https://rss.sciencedirect.com/publication/science/02642751) and [Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence](https://rss.sciencedirect.com/publication/science/2666920X). Archive permitted feed snapshots incrementally: current item descriptions can show an assigned future issue month instead of their earlier first-online date. A monthly snapshot is not assured to retain a complete historical window. The Crolla lead currently shows December 2026 in the official feed, while the secondary mirror retains an asserted 19 August first-online date.
- Exclude first-online-outside-window issue assignments. [The Manchester institutional record for Guo/Schuldenfrei's sensor-placement paper](https://research.manchester.ac.uk/en/publications/physics-informed-multi-objective-placement-of-indoor-multi-modal-/) explicitly lists early online 30 June 2026 and issue publication 1 September; only the latter falls inside this review window.
- Repository and publisher feeds have disciplinary and indexing gaps. Report “no dated in-window item found in checked sources,” not “no publications” or “no productivity.” This bounded pilot did not establish exhaustive publication coverage for every roster member.
